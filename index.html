Privacy

Customers: we ask for your name and phone number. They are saved on your phone. When you contact a fundi on WhatsApp, your name and number are sent to that fundi in the message.

Fundis: we keep your name, trade, area, phone number and any photos you choose to share, so customers can find you. We ask for a photo of your valid ID to check who you are. We check it and then delete it.

We do not sell your information. To ask us to remove your details, contact us from Settings.
